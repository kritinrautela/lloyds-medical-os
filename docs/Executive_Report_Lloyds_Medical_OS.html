01 Executive Mission, Operating Context & Problem Statement
STRATEGIC CONTEXT

Remote mineral exploration concessions, open-pit mines, and extraction assets operated by Lloyds Metals & Energy Limited (LMEL) in Papua New Guinea (e.g. Morobe Province, Bulolo Valley, Markham River Basin, and Highland Ridges) face extreme environmental, technical, and human factors challenges:

The Rural Concession Reality
  • Zero or Severed Internet: Highland cloud cover, jungle storms, and generator fuel shortages frequently knock out satellite and cellular links for days or weeks.
  • Acute Clinical Hazards: Remote crews face Falciparum malaria, venomous snakebites (Papuan Taipan / Death Adder), heavy equipment blast/crush trauma, and acute dehydration.
The Operational & Financial Failure
  • Standard SaaS Cloud EHRs Fail: Epic, Cerner, or web portals freeze the moment internet drops, forcing staff back to illegible paper notebooks.
  • Inventory & Cash Pilferage: Without real-time stock-to-patient linkage and daily cash reconciliation, vital antibiotics disappear and clinic revenues vanish.

The Lloyds Medical Operating System (LMOS-PNG) was engineered from the ground up to conquer these constraints. It is an entirely self-contained digital hospital platform that runs directly from a high-speed USB flash drive on any clinic PC or laptop with zero external database installation and zero internet required.

To eliminate patient file confusion in remote areas where workers and village members may share similar names, the system automatically assigns an immutable date-stamped identifier upon check-in: LMEL-[YEAR][MONTH][DAY]-[SEQUENCE] (e.g. LMEL-20260904-008). This guarantees chronological traceability across years of operations.

A shift report is not legally finalized until physically reviewed and signed by:

  3. Official Concession Stamp: Affixed in the designated stamp box to prevent counterfeit report substitution.

15 Standard Operating Procedures (Clinic SOP Runbook)
DAILY CLINIC SOP

PHASE 1: 06:00 MORNING SHIFT OPENING

1. Insert USB Pen Drive and double-click Start_Hospital_Windows.bat.
2. Open Chrome to http://localhost:4000.
3. Log into Command Center with Doctor/Nurse credentials.
4. Review Pharmacy Formulary for low-stock alarms.

PHASE 2: PATIENT INTAKE & TRIAGE

1. Click "Quick Check-in" for new or returning patient.
2. System generates unique ID LMEL-YYYYMMDD-XXX.
3. Enter vital signs (BP, SpO2, HR, Temp) & assign Manchester triage priority.
4. Patient automatically appears in Doctor OPD Queue.

PHASE 3: DOCTOR CONSULTATION

1. Select patient from active OPD Queue.
2. Review vitals trend and enter clinical notes & diagnosis.
3. Prescribe essential medications with dosage instructions.
4. Click "Save & Route to Pharmacy".

PHASE 4: PHARMACY DISPENSATION & POS

1. Open Dispense POS and select patient name.
2. Confirm prescribed drugs, quantities, and batch numbers.
3. Collect fee in PNG Kina (PGK) and print bilingual invoice receipt.
4. Stock quantities decrement automatically in real time.

PHASE 5: 18:00 EVENING SHIFT CLOSEOUT & SAFE EJECT

1. Navigate to End-of-Day tab.
2. Count physical cash in safe/drawer and enter total.
3. Confirm variance reads exactly 0.00 BALANCED.
4. Enter handover notes and click "Close & Finalize Today's Shift".
5. Click "Print Shift Audit Report", sign, and affix official concession circular stamp.
6. Click "Safe Pen Drive Eject" to flush SQLite WAL buffers and safely unplug the drive.
